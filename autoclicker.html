IYM Autoclicker

Lightweight automation tool for macOS

GET

Do you like playing games? If you like playing games, you'll love IYM Autoclicker! This app can help you automate repetitive clicking work, and is very helpful for your game playing.

That's why I built IYM Autoclicker. It's a natively designed macOS utility that does exactly one thing, and does it perfectly: it clicks for you.

Why choose IYM Autoclicker?

  • Lightweight: It barely uses any CPU or memory.
  • Precision: Set exact coordinates and millisecond intervals.
  • UI: Beautiful UI made with two styles to choose from.
