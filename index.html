Why I created MQA MacLite?

🧑‍💻 The Beginning

When I was 14, I became a Mac user.(Also, I'm 14 years old now! ) I tried many apps and spent time on GitHub exploring, but found that many were heavy and useless.

🛠️ The Solution

So, I decided to take matters into my own hands. I created this website to build and find lightweight, useful apps for users like you.

🎯 The Goal

MQA MacLite aims to provide a minimalist, efficient, and hassle-free experience. If you love lightweight tools, try my projects and share your feedback!
